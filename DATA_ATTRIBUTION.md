# Data Attribution

The source code in this repository is released under the MIT License. Data files and derived datasets remain subject to the terms of their original providers.

## Primary Sources

- European Central Bank, Survey on the Access to Finance of Enterprises (SAFE): https://data.ecb.europa.eu/
- European Central Bank, New Composite Indicator of Systemic Stress (New CISS): https://data.ecb.europa.eu/
- European Central Bank, Bank Lending Survey and MFI Interest Rate Statistics: https://data.ecb.europa.eu/
- Eurostat, business demography and short-term business statistics: https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/
- World Bank, World Development Indicators: https://data.worldbank.org/

The repository includes selected raw extracts and processed analytical outputs for reproducibility. Redistribution or reuse of those data should follow each provider's current terms and citation guidance.
